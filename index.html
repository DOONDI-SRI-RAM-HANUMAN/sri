About the farm

Growing Quality With Care

Sridhar Poultry is a working poultry farm based in Morusumilli Village, Andhra Pradesh. Our day-to-day focus is simple: look after the birds properly, keep the farm clean, and stay dependable for the people who buy from us.

  • Bird care first — daily monitoring, comfortable housing and attentive handling.
  • Farm cleanliness — routine cleaning, dry litter management and controlled access.
  • Quality feed & water — consistent feeding schedules and clean drinking water.
  • Reliable operations — planned batches so we can answer availability honestly.
  • Straightforward service — clear communication with households and businesses alike.
